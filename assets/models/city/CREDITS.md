# City asset credits

Imported 21 building GLBs from Kenney City Kit Suburban and 10 modular road GLBs from Kenney City Kit Roads, copied from the CC0 collection at https://github.com/petroulacl/fps-buildings-env-kit.

Original author: Kenney (https://kenney.nl/assets).
License: CC0 1.0 Public Domain. The original source collection identifies these packs as CC0.
The matching `Textures/colormap.png` atlases are kept beside each model family because the GLBs reference them by relative path.

These are stylized low-poly game assets, not photorealistic scans. They are being used as mobile-friendly modular city geometry.
