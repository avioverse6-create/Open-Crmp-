# Third-party character models

These five GLB files are copied from the `glassback/models/` folder of
[devvinish/vinish-games-assets](https://github.com/devvinish/vinish-games-assets).

- `citizen_bryce.glb`, `citizen_sophie.glb`, `citizen_remy.glb`, and `citizen_shannon.glb`: Mixamo character models (Adobe Mixamo), bundled and converted to GLB by vinish.dev.
- `mixamo_animation_library.glb`: Mixamo animation clips bundled and converted to GLB by vinish.dev. Clips include `idle`, `run`, `back`, `side_l`, `side_r`, `strafe_l`, `strafe_r`, `cheer`, and `sad`.

The source project's `glassback/CREDITS.md` identifies the characters and motions as Mixamo assets and states they are royalty-free for use inside games under Adobe's General Terms and Mixamo FAQ. They are not CC0 and must not be redistributed as a standalone asset pack. This repository includes them only as bundled game assets; retain this notice and review Adobe's current terms before publishing a build.

Source credits: [vinish.dev](https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models), [Adobe Mixamo](https://www.mixamo.com/).
